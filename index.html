Questions

Is the terminal really free?

Yes. The full terminal runs in your browser without an account. Holder features will be added on top, nothing that is free today gets taken away.

Where does the data come from?

Public news coverage, exchange APIs and market data feeds, processed by our own engine. Data can be delayed or briefly unavailable when a source is down.

How do I know I have the real contract address?

Only trust the address shown on meridian-gmi.com and posted by our official X account. We will never DM you first and never ask for your seed phrase.

Is this financial advice?

No. Meridian shows where risk is building and how markets have reacted. What you do with that is your call.
